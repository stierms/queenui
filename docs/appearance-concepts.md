# QueenUI appearance concepts — gallery draft

These four pairings were approved and promoted into the Settings catalog.
They remain in the DEV gallery alongside the earlier Relay, Basalt, Monotype
and Optic identities for grid- and Focus-scale comparison.

## Switchgear / Contact

**Thesis:** A putty-enamel switchboard carries porcelain and black-bakelite
relay chessmen, making the game feel like durable control equipment in an
operations console.

- **Board:** putty enamel light `#aaa39a`; hard-rubber dark `#3b332f`.
  Last move is a pair of thin nickel contact bars (`#c9c2b8`), with no fill.
  Check is claret `#dd7a6f`. Coordinates are `#403936` on light and `#ddd5ca`
  on dark.
- **Pieces — Contact:** pawns and officers are stacks of ceramic insulator discs;
  rooks have three contact-block merlons and winding ribs; the horse has a
  long terminal muzzle, a single ear, jaw, eye, comb-like mane and a coil-wound
  chest. White is glazed porcelain with a dry umber edge. Black is bakelite
  with pale incisions and the bone-grey halo rim. Both colours use the same
  mould.
- **Why QueenUI:** relays, contacts and switchboards are the physical
  vocabulary of supervised machinery. The material makes sense beside engine
  state and telemetry without borrowing an online-tournament board.

## Kiln / Shard

**Thesis:** A fired-clay chequer carries pressed stoneware pieces whose
cleavage planes stay legible like heat-resistant shop furniture, not polished
marble.

- **Board:** fired clay light `#b78368`; manganese-slip dark `#3a2e2a`. Last
  move is a pale sgraffito incision along two edges (`#d8b9a5`), not a wash.
  Check is claret `#dd7a6f`. Coordinates are `#493128` on light and `#e4c5b2`
  on dark.
- **Pieces — Shard:** biscuit and oxide stoneware share one angular press
  mould. Heads and stems break into broad planes; the rook has three square
  merlons and two courses; the bishop has a deep diagonal cleft; the queen has
  a cut-stone coronet; and every piece lands on the same chipped three-course
  plinth. Incisions are matte, not glossy highlights.
- **Why QueenUI:** kiln furniture is compact, tough and made to survive long
  production runs—the right vernacular for four rapid games running for hours.
  Terracotta also explores stone without repeating the grey Marble theme.

## Compositor / Sort

**Thesis:** A composing-stone board carries punch-cut hot-metal type sorts,
bringing QueenUI's Plex Mono working language onto the board as hardware.

- **Board:** composing-stone light `#c9c4b9`; ink-violet matrix dark `#38343d`.
  Last move is four printer's registration corners (`#8b8490`), with the square
  left unfilled. Check is claret `#dd7a6f`. Coordinates are `#46404a` on light
  and `#d8d1d9` on dark.
- **Pieces — Sort:** the silhouettes are squared punches with slab terminals,
  rectangular collars, deliberate ink traps and a common type-block foot. The
  rook carries three merlons and two rules; the bishop's mitre is an open
  diagonal counter; the queen uses square punch mills; the horse has a clear
  profile, ear, block muzzle, jaw and stepped mane. White is chalked type metal;
  black is inked alloy with the required halo.
- **Why QueenUI:** the console already uses Plex Mono for moves and telemetry.
  Type sorts turn that job-specific typography into a physical piece language,
  rather than applying a font-themed colour skin.

## Paperwhite / Aperture

**Thesis:** A high-contrast electrophoretic board carries diaphragm-cut
chessmen built to remain distinct in an eight-board operations grid.

- **Board:** e-paper light `#b5b2a8`; carbon dark `#272927`. Last move is four
  short terminal brackets (`#d7d8cf`) with no glow or fill. Check is claret
  `#dd7a6f`. Coordinates are `#3e423e` on light and `#d7d9d0` on dark.
- **Pieces — Aperture:** broad flat pigment shapes use large counters instead
  of shading: a ring pawn head, viewing-slot rook, cleft diaphragm bishop,
  three-mill queen and pierced king body. The horse has one ear, a blunt muzzle,
  jaw, eye aperture, stepped mane, chest and shallow base. White and black are
  the same cut; black keeps a light e-paper halo.
- **Why QueenUI:** electrophoretic displays privilege persistent, low-fatigue
  state and work without ornamental light. The oversized counters and shallow
  feet are specifically tuned for the 40–52px range of dense bot operations.
